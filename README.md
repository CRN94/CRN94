## The Impossible Laboratory

I am building **Machina Arcana**, a private open world for turning curiosity into
understanding, instruments, and defensible artifacts.

The subjects move around—mathematics, artificial intelligence, markets,
computation, physics, and philosophy—but the method stays recognizable:

```text
encounter -> wager -> mechanism -> assay -> residue -> return
```

The theatrical premise is an auto-generative laboratory of alchemy and
numeromancy. I have no idea where any of this is going - and that's by design.

## At the workbench

These are live investigations, not claims of finished software.

| Working | The question | Honest state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A Miniature Island** | How does a sequenced stream of limit orders become a market? | Historical sources and the first price-time-priority wager are staged; the matching engine has not been built yet. |
| **Bitcoin: Keys, Work, and the Quantum Boundary** | What actually creates spendable ownership, and which assumptions does a quantum computer threaten? | The first public-key-exposure puzzle is staged; implementation has not begun. |
| **The Revenue State Machine** | How do noisy commercial events become defensible claims about account state and next action? | The first authority question is staged using synthetic events; no live CRM or client data is connected. |
| **Sovereign AI** | What changes when models run on hardware I own—and when I open the forward pass? | Hardware notes, a model-dissection ladder, and a local-inference measurement harness exist privately. |
| **Fat Tails** | At finite sample size, which observation owns the conclusion? | The founding question and primary source are preserved; the first experiment remains to be chosen. |

The strongest current collision is among three machines that turn events into
state: a Bitcoin network, an electronic market, and a revenue organization.
Their resemblance is a hypothesis to test, not a conclusion to advertise.

## Working doctrine

- Make a prediction before a consequential reveal.
- Prefer a tiny executable mechanism to a large unexplained system.
- Separate what is proved, observed, inferred, analogous, speculative, and
  fictional.
- Let code, equations, diagrams, prose, and tools disagree in public.
- Use AI for search, plumbing, implementation, and hostile review; retain human
  ownership of the mechanism and the claim.
- Publish failures and counterexamples when they change the map.

## What I am building toward

1. A small public executable whose rules can be reconstructed line by line.
2. A reproducible **Field Report from the Impossible Laboratory** containing
   the wager, mechanism, failed attempts, evidence, and surviving question.
3. A growing public constellation of tools and reports—only after each one
   earns an independent lifecycle outside the private laboratory.

No fake roadmap, no contribution-count theater, and no claim that a private
notebook is already a public body of work. The visible profile should grow at
the speed of inspectable residue.
